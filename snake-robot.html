Simulation Architecture & Core Subsystems

Subsystem 1

Joint_Angles Block

Generates serpenoid traveling wave commands: θ = A·sin(Ωn + ωt), transitioning from straight platform takeoff to airborne lateral undulation.

Subsystem 2

Servo_PD Controller

Simulates internal motor position tracking with torque limits matching physical FEETECH FS90-FB micro servos (τ = 0.074 N·m).

Subsystem 3

Multi-Body Dynamics

Solves non-linear Lagrangian mass matrix M(q) and Coriolis matrix C(q, q̇) coupled with dynamic aerodynamic force vectors.

Subsystem 4

Forward Kinematics

Maps inter-module joint coordinates into absolute global Cartesian space, referencing center-of-mass progression with symmetric bilateral waves.

Simulation Predictions vs. Empirical Results

1. Velocity Threshold Effect

Simulations revealed that low airspeeds provide insufficient flight time for undulation to generate net aerodynamic lift benefits. However, at higher launch speeds (1.8 m/s), physical trials confirmed an 8.7% increase in mean horizontal glide distance (from 0.595 m to 0.647 m, p = 0.010).

2. Optimal Amplitude Window

Parametric amplitude sweeps (0° to 30°) demonstrated that 10° maximized glide distance at high velocities. Larger amplitudes caused induced drag to overtake lift benefits, underscoring the critical balance between joint deflection and aerodynamic efficiency.
