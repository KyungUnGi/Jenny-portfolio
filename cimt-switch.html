Pediatric Assistive Technology & Biomedical Device

CIMT Assistive Switch Device

• Clinical Validation Prototype

A non-restrictive therapeutic switch system for children with cerebral palsy, promoting motor recovery and intentional non-dominant hand engagement through interactive wireless play.

SolidWorks CAD 3D Printing (PLA) Hall Effect Magnetic Sensing 433 MHz RF Wireless Link Ergonomic Iteration Pediatric CIMT Therapy

Project Overview

Constraint-Induced Movement Therapy (CIMT) effectively improves motor outcomes in children with cerebral palsy by promoting non-dominant arm use. However, conventional interventions rely on physical casting or rigid binding, which causes high frustration and sensory discomfort.

  • Sensory-Friendly Alternative: Replaces rigid casts with an open-palm rest switch and a playful magnetic wearable strap.
  • Reward-Driven Logic: Internal Hall effect sensor continuously triggers power to an interactive sensory board while the dominant hand remains placed.
  • Clinical Guidance: Developed in close partnership with clinical contact Virginia Quellmalz-Zallocco, OTD/S, COTA, CLT.
System Operational Pipeline
1. Contact Magnetic strap rests on mouse dome
2. RF Relay 433 MHz signal unlocks power
3. Active Play Non-dominant hand operates board

Functional Validation & Demonstration

Demonstration of the closed-loop switch system: lifting the dominant hand immediately breaks the magnetic threshold (2–3 cm) and cuts circuit power, motivating the child to maintain posture while exploring the sensory board.

Physical Design Evolution

Prototype 1

Vertical Peg Grasp Switch

Initial 3D-printed iteration featuring a vertical cylindrical handle. Clinical evaluation revealed that pediatric patients with spasticity could not comfortably grasp or open clenched fingers around the upright post.

Prototype 2 & 3

Ergonomic Mouse Dome (Final)

Pivoted to a low-profile curved mouse geometry. The contoured dome supports relaxed palm resting without forcing finger extension, housing the internal magnetic sensor cleanly below the surface.

Custom Toy Modification & Safety Enclosure

SolidWorks CAD enclosure designed for master power interface

3D-printed protective faceplate installed on sensory board

The interactive busy board was retrofitted with custom 3D-printed protective shielding to isolate the battery terminals and wire splicing, ensuring full durability and safety for continuous pediatric handling.
